## Test protocol

1. Choose the paper and check the official duration.
2. Write the start and finish times.
3. Work without notes, internet, calculator, or hints.
4. Submit every written attempt for correction.
5. Do not immediately read solutions after finishing.
6. Complete a correction session before taking the next full paper.
