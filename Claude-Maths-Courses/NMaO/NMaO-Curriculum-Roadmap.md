# NMaO Curriculum Roadmap

<span class="nmao-badge nmao-study">CURRICULUM</span>

> [!important] The main pathway
> We learn the general toolkit first, practise it on non-target problems, use mocks to measure readiness, and then climb through the NMaO papers from TC toward 1BAC.

See [[NMaO-Lesson-Index]] for the complete lesson-by-lesson sequence.

## The complete cycle

1. **Pre-learn** the concepts and vocabulary.
2. **Learn** the central methods through examples.
3. **Train** on easy and medium problems.
4. **Mini-mock** without help.
5. **Correct** and record recurring errors.
6. **Full mock** at the current level.
7. **Advance** only when the evidence supports it.

## Phase 0 - Diagnostic

- Paper observed: TC 2017-2018 Test 1
- Result: 2 of 4 problems attempted, with uncertainty and long time use
- Purpose: identify gaps, not assign a permanent level
- Action: keep the correction, then return to it after the foundation phase

## Phase 1 - General TC foundation

### Block A: Algebra and inequalities

- Algebraic identities and factorisation
- Fractions and common denominators
- Equations and systems
- Symmetric expressions
- AM-GM and equality cases
- Basic Cauchy-Schwarz and rearrangement ideas
- Polynomials and useful substitutions

### Block B: Number theory

- Divisibility and prime factorisation
- Greatest common divisors and the Euclidean algorithm
- Remainders and congruences
- Parity
- Diophantine equations
- Prime-number arguments

### Block C: Geometry

- Angle chasing
- Congruence and similarity
- Pythagoras and power of a point
- Cyclic quadrilaterals
- Midpoints and homothety
- Coordinates and squared distances
- Vectors, projections, and transformations

### Block D: Combinatorics and proof

- Counting principles
- Pigeonhole principle
- Invariants and monovariants
- Extremal arguments
- Recursion and sequences
- Mathematical induction
- Contradiction, cases, and clean proof-writing

## Block study standard

For each block:

- [ ] Learn the definitions and core identities
- [ ] Understand 2-3 worked examples
- [ ] Solve 5 basic problems
- [ ] Solve 3 intermediate problems
- [ ] Complete a mini-mock without notes
- [ ] Correct every mistake
- [ ] Re-solve one failed problem after a delay

## Phase 2 - TC level climb

### TC Foundation benchmark

Use an unseen 2017-2018 paper as a full mock after Phase 1. Target: meaningful progress on most problems and at least two complete, logically sound solutions.

### TC Intermediate

Use the 2018-2019 and 2019-2020 papers. Mix focused practice with timed sections. Advance after two or three mocks show consistent improvement.

### TC Advanced

Use the 2020-2021 through 2025-2026 papers. Complete full papers under their official durations, then perform full corrections.

## Phase 3 - 1BAC bridge and 1BAC

Add:

- Stronger inequalities and equality analysis
- More difficult polynomial and functional problems
- Floor functions and advanced Diophantine equations
- Harder geometry and multi-step configurations
- More sophisticated invariants and extremal reasoning

Then use the 1BAC papers as level benchmarks and exam simulations.
