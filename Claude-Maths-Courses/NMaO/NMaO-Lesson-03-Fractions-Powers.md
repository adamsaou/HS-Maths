# Lesson 03 - Fractions, Powers, and Algebraic Simplification

<span class="nmao-badge nmao-study">LESSON</span>

## Objective

Manipulate powers and algebraic fractions safely, while tracking restrictions before cancelling.

## 1. Laws of powers

For nonzero $a$ and integers $m,n$:

$$
a^m\cdot a^n=a^{m+n},
$$

$$
\frac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n},
$$

$$
(a^m)^n=a^{mn},
$$

$$
(ab)^n=a^n b^n,
$$

and

$$
a^{-n}=\frac1{a^n}.
$$

Also:

$$
a^0=1\qquad(a\ne0).
$$

## 2. Simplifying powers

Example:

$$
\frac{x^3\cdot x^5}{x^2}
=x^{3+5-2}
=x^6.
$$

With several variables:

$$
\frac{a^{-2}b^3}{ab^{-1}}
=a^{-3}b^4
=\frac{b^4}{a^3}.
$$

## 3. Algebraic fractions

Always factor before cancelling.

Example:

$$
\frac{x^2-9}{x^2+3x}
=\frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{x(x+3)}
=\frac{x-3}{x}.
$$

The original denominator requires

$$
x\ne0,\qquad x+3\ne0.
$$

Therefore the simplification is valid only for $x\ne0,-3$.

## 4. The cancellation rule

You may cancel a factor, not a term.

Correct:

$$
\frac{(x+2)(x-1)}{x+2}=x-1
\qquad(x\ne-2).
$$

Incorrect:

$$
\frac{x+2}{x}\ne2.
$$

The numerator and denominator do not have a common factor $x$.

## 5. Useful factorisation before simplification

For example:

$$
\frac{x^2-4}{x^2-4x+4}
=\frac{(x-2)(x+2)}{(x-2)^2}
=\frac{x+2}{x-2},
$$

with the original restriction $x\ne2$.

- [ ] Factor numerators and denominators.

- [ ] Cancel factors only.

## Practice

### Basic

**A.** Simplify:

$$
\frac{x^7\cdot x^2}{x^4}.
$$

**B.** Rewrite with positive exponents:

$$
\frac{a^{-3}b^2}{c^{-2}}.
$$

**C.** Simplify and state the restrictions:

$$
\frac{x^2-25}{x^2+5x}.
$$

### Intermediate

**D.** Simplify and state all restrictions:

$$
\frac{x^2-4x+4}{x^2-4}.
$$

**E.** Simplify:

$$
\frac{a^2-b^2}{a^2+2ab+b^2}.
$$

**F.** Explain why this cancellation is invalid:

$$
\frac{x+5}{x}=5.
$$

Then give a correct simplification, if possible.
