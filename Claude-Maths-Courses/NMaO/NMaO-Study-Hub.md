## The system

1. **Learn** one technique or idea.
2. **Train** it on focused problems.
3. **Attempt** mixed problems independently.
4. **Test** under timed exam conditions.
5. **Correct** every attempt deeply.
6. **Review** the correction queue before learning new material.

See [[NMaO-Study-System]], [[NMaO-Session-Template]], [[NMaO-Testing-Log]], and [[NMaO-Corrections]].

## Advancement rules

- Stay in **TC foundations** until the core topic checklist is reasonably secure.
- Move to **stronger TC** after three timed papers show consistent progress and mostly complete proofs.
- Move to **1BAC** after reaching about 60% of a TC paper under time pressure, with no major proof-writing weakness.
- A topic is not mastered until it can be used in a new problem without a worked example.

## Paper progression

We first learn the general TC toolkit, then use unseen papers as mocks, and only then climb through the archive. The 2017-2018 Test 1 attempt is diagnostic evidence, not a completed paper.
