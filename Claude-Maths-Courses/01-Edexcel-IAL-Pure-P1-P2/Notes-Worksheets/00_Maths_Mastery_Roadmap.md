# Maths Mastery Roadmap — Edexcel International A-Level Pure 1 & 2

**Goal:** Master the full content of *Pure Mathematics 1 (P1)* and *Pure Mathematics 2 (P2)* in ~12 weeks, to an "impressive, exercises-done-flawlessly" standard for your Harrow application.

**Your starting point:** Tronc commun (Moroccan Year 10). You already have solid algebra, basic geometry, and equation-solving. That covers a good chunk of P1 Chapters 1–3. We build straight up from there.

**The honest truth about "flawless":** Flawless does not come from understanding — it comes from *volume of correct repetitions*. Understanding gets you to "I can do it slowly with hints." Drilling gets you to "I can do it fast, alone, under pressure, without errors." This plan is built around that.

---

## How to use this plan (read once, then live by it)

**Daily session (~2.5–3 hrs, 6 days/week):**

1. **Warm-up recall (15 min).** Redo 3–4 problems from *previous* days from memory. No notes. This is the single highest-value habit — it fights forgetting.
2. **Learn (40 min).** Read the topic notes for today's sub-topic. Copy each worked example onto paper *yourself* — do not just read it. Then close the notes and reproduce one example from scratch.
3. **Drill (60–80 min).** Do the practice set for that sub-topic. Work in pen, show every line.
4. **Mark & log (20 min).** Check answers immediately. Every mistake goes in an **Error Log** (see below). A problem you got wrong is re-attempted the next day in warm-up.

**The Error Log (your secret weapon):** A notebook with three columns — *Problem / What I did wrong / The fix in one line*. Before any test, you re-do every problem in your error log. This is how top students get to flawless.

**Mastery checkpoint per chapter:** You "own" a chapter when you can do a mixed exercise of 15 problems, cold, in one sitting, scoring 14/15+ with no notes. Not before. If you fall short, you re-drill, you don't move on. Speed comes after accuracy — first be correct, then be fast.

## The 12-week schedule

Weeks 1–6 cover **P1**. Weeks 7–11 cover **P2**. Week 12 is full mixed review and timed practice.

### Phase 1 — Pure 1 (Weeks 1–6)

| Week | Focus | Chapters |
|------|-------|----------|
| **1** | Algebra foundations | Ch 1 Algebraic expressions (indices, surds, rationalising) · Ch 2 Quadratics |
| **2** | Equations, graphs | Ch 3 Equations & inequalities · Ch 4 Graphs & transformations |
| **3** | Coordinate geometry & trig start | Ch 5 Straight-line graphs · Ch 6 Trigonometric ratios (sine/cosine rule, areas, graphs) |
| **4** | Trig + calculus start | Ch 7 Trig identities & equations · begin Ch 8 Differentiation |
| **5** | Calculus | Finish Ch 8 Differentiation · Ch 9 Integration |
| **6** | **P1 consolidation** | Mixed P1 exercises + first timed past paper. Re-drill weak chapters from Error Log. |

### Phase 2 — Pure 2 (Weeks 7–11)

| Week | Focus | Chapters (P2) |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **7** | Algebraic methods & proof | Algebraic fractions, polynomial division, factor & remainder theorems, proof |
| **8** | Circles & logs | Coordinate geometry (circles) · Exponentials & logarithms |
| **9** | Series | The binomial expansion · Sequences & series (arithmetic & geometric) |
| **10** | Trig & calculus extended | Radian measure, arc length & sector area · extended differentiation |
| **11** | **P2 consolidation** | Extended integration · mixed P2 exercises + timed past paper |

*(P2 chapter order will be fine-tuned against your exact book once I can read the file — but the topics above are the full P2 syllabus and won't change.)*

### Phase 3 — Lock it in (Week 12)

- 2 full timed past papers (P1 and P2), marked strictly against the mark scheme.
- Re-do your **entire Error Log** from the 11 weeks.
- Drill only your weakest 2–3 topics to flawless.

## Milestones you can feel

- **End of Week 2:** You can factorise, complete the square, use the discriminant, and solve any quadratic or simultaneous system without thinking. This alone already looks strong.
- **End of Week 5:** You can differentiate and integrate polynomials and use them to find tangents, stationary points, and areas. This is the part that signals "A-level maths," not GCSE.
- **End of Week 11:** Full P1 + P2 coverage, every topic at mastery checkpoint.
- **Week 12:** Past papers at a confident, comfortable score.

## If you fall behind

Don't compress the drilling — compress the *new content*. It is far better to deeply own 80% of the syllabus than to have shakily seen 100%. Cut Week 10–11's hardest extensions before you ever cut practice volume.
