# Worksheet 1 — Algebraic Methods

1. ⭐ Simplify `(x² − 4)/(x² − x − 6)`
2. ⭐ Find the remainder when `f(x) = x³ + 2x² − 3x + 4` is divided by `(x − 2)`.
3. ⭐⭐ Show `(x − 1)` is a factor of `x³ − 3x² − x + 3` and factorise it fully.
4. ⭐ Find the remainder when `2x³ − x² + 5` is divided by `(x + 1)`.
5. ⭐⭐ Factorise fully `x³ − 4x² + x + 6`.

**Answers 1:** 1) `(x−2)/(x−3)`  2) `f(2)=8+8−6+4=14`  3) `f(1)=0` ✓; `(x−1)(x−3)(x+1)`  4) `f(−1)=−2−1+5=2`  5) `f(2)=0` ⇒ `(x−2)(x²−2x−3)=(x−2)(x−3)(x+1)`

# Worksheet 2 — Circles

1. ⭐ State the centre and radius of `(x − 2)² + (y + 5)² = 49`.
2. ⭐⭐ Find the centre and radius of `x² + y² − 6x + 4y − 12 = 0`.
3. ⭐⭐ Find the centre and radius of `x² + y² + 8x − 2y + 8 = 0`.
4. ⭐⭐⭐ A circle has centre `(3, −2)`. Find the equation of the tangent at the point `P(6, 2)` on the circle, in the form `ax + by + c = 0`.

**Answers 2:** 1) centre `(2, −5)`, radius `7`  2) `(x−3)²+(y+2)²=25` → centre `(3, −2)`, radius `5`  3) `(x+4)²+(y−1)²=9` → centre `(−4, 1)`, radius `3`  4) radius gradient `4/3`, tangent gradient `−3/4`: `3x + 4y − 26 = 0`

# Worksheet 3 — Exponentials and Logarithms

1. ⭐⭐ Solve `2ˣ = 10` (3 s.f.).
2. ⭐ Solve `5ˣ = 0.2`.
3. ⭐ Write `2 log 3 + log 2` as a single logarithm.
4. ⭐⭐ Solve `log₂ x + log₂(x − 2) = 3`.
5. ⭐⭐ Solve `e^{2x} = 7` (3 s.f.).

**Answers 3:** 1) `x = ln10/ln2 ≈ 3.32`  2) `0.2 = 5⁻¹ ⇒ x = −1`  3) `log 18`  4) `log₂[x(x−2)]=3 ⇒ x²−2x=8 ⇒ (x−4)(x+2)=0 ⇒ x = 4` (reject −2)  5) `2x = ln7 ⇒ x ≈ 0.973`

# Worksheet 5 — Sequences and Series

1. ⭐ Arithmetic, first term `5`, common difference `3`. Find the 20th term and `S₂₀`.
2. ⭐ Geometric, `a = 3`, `r = 2`. Find the 6th term and `S₆`.
3. ⭐⭐ Geometric, `a = 12`, `r = ⅓`. Find the sum to infinity.
4. ⭐⭐⭐ In an arithmetic sequence the 7th term is 23 and the 12th term is 38. Find `a` and `d`.

**Answers 5:** 1) `u₂₀ = 5 + 19·3 = 62`; `S₂₀ = 10(10 + 57) = 670`  2) `u₆ = 3·2⁵ = 96`; `S₆ = 3(2⁶−1)/1 = 189`  3) `12/(1 − ⅓) = 18`  4) `a+6d=23`, `a+11d=38` ⇒ `5d=15 ⇒ d=3, a=5`

# Worksheet 6 — Radian Measure

1. ⭐ Convert `135°` to radians (exact).
2. ⭐ A sector has `r = 10`, `θ = 0.6` rad. Find the arc length and area.
3. ⭐ Convert `5π/6` radians to degrees.
4. ⭐⭐ A sector of radius `4` has area `24 cm²`. Find `θ` and the arc length.

**Answers 6:** 1) `3π/4`  2) arc `= 10(0.6) = 6`; area `= ½(100)(0.6) = 30`  3) `150°`  4) `24 = ½(16)θ ⇒ θ = 3` rad; arc `= rθ = 12 cm`

# Worksheet 7 — Differentiation: Applications & Optimisation

1. ⭐⭐ Find the stationary points of `y = x³ − 6x² + 9x` and their nature.
2. ⭐⭐ A farmer has `60 m` of fencing for three sides of a rectangular field (a wall forms the fourth side). Find the maximum area.
3. ⭐⭐ The sum of two positive numbers is `12`. Find their maximum product.

**Answers 7:** 1) `f'=3(x−1)(x−3)=0 ⇒ x=1,3`; `f''=6x−12`: max `(1, 4)`, min `(3, 0)`  2) `2x + y = 60 ⇒ A = x(60−2x)=60x−2x²`; `A'=60−4x=0 ⇒ x=15, y=30`; max area `= 450 m²` (`A''=−4<0`)  3) `P = x(12−x)`; `P'=12−2x=0 ⇒ x=6`; max product `= 36`

# Worksheet 8 — Integration: Area & the Trapezium Rule

1. ⭐ Evaluate `∫₁³ (4x − 1) dx`.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ Find the area enclosed by `y = x²` and `y = x + 2`.
3. ⭐⭐ Use the trapezium rule with 4 strips to estimate `∫₀² (x² + 1) dx`. Compare with the exact value.
4. ⭐⭐ Use the trapezium rule with 2 strips to estimate `∫₁² (1/x) dx`.

**Answers 8:** 1) `[2x²−x]₁³ = 15 − 1 = 14`  2) intersect `x=−1,2`; `∫₋₁²[(x+2)−x²]dx = [x²/2+2x−x³/3]₋₁² = 10/3 −(−7/6) = 4.5`  3) `h=0.5`, ordinates `1, 1.25, 2, 3.25, 5`: `≈ 0.25[1+5+2(1.25+2+3.25)] = 4.75`; exact `= 14/3 ≈ 4.667` (slight overestimate)  4) `h=0.5`, ordinates `1, 0.6667, 0.5`: `≈ 0.25[1+0.5+2(0.6667)] = 0.708`; exact `ln2 ≈ 0.693`
