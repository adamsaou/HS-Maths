# Claude maths courses

This folder collects the maths courses and related materials found in Claude Cowork and the existing maths folders.

## Folders

- `NMaO` — National and international mathematics olympiad papers, organized by olympiad, year, and paper type.

- `01-Edexcel-IAL-Pure-P1-P2` — Edexcel International A-Level Pure Mathematics 1 and 2, including the 12-week roadmap, notes, worksheets, HTML lesson books, and source PDFs.
- `02-1BAC-Sciences-Mathematiques` — 1ère Bac Sciences Mathématiques course, topic PDFs, diagrams, generated exercises, scripts, and the course archive.
- `03-Olympiad-and-Additional-Maths` — Math Maroc Olympiad booklet, Terence Tao's problem-solving book, and other additional maths PDFs/images.
- `04-Math-ML-From-Scratch` — the linear-algebra/math-from-scratch project.
